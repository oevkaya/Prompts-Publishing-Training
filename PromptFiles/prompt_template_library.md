## Book Concept Development

### Template 1: Book Idea Generation
```
You are a creative publishing strategist with expertise in [CATEGORY]. 
Generate [NUMBER] innovative book concepts for [GENRE/TOPIC] that would 
appeal to [TARGET AUDIENCE]. 

For each concept, provide:
1. A working title
2. One-sentence hook
3. Target audience
4. Unique angle or differentiator

Focus on [SPECIFIC CRITERIA, e.g., contemporary challenges, emerging trends].
```

**Variables to customize:**
- CATEGORY: non-fiction, fiction, academic, children's, etc.
- NUMBER: 5-15 concepts
- GENRE/TOPIC: specific area of interest
- TARGET AUDIENCE: demographics and psychographics
- SPECIFIC CRITERIA: what makes concepts relevant

## Quality Control

### Template 19: Fact-Checking Request
```
Review this content for factual accuracy:

[PASTE CONTENT]

For each factual claim:
1. Identify the claim
2. Assess verifiability (can it be checked?)
3. Note if sources are provided
4. Flag claims that seem questionable
5. Suggest verification methods

Provide:
- List of all factual claims
- Risk assessment (high/medium/low) for each
- Recommended verification steps
- Any claims that should be removed or qualified
```

## Advanced Techniques

### Template 22: Chain-of-Thought Prompting
```
Let's think through [PROBLEM/QUESTION] step by step:

[DESCRIBE PROBLEM OR PASTE CONTENT]

Step 1: [FIRST ANALYSIS DIMENSION]
[What should be considered? What data is relevant?]

Step 2: [SECOND ANALYSIS DIMENSION]
[What are the implications? What patterns emerge?]

Step 3: [THIRD ANALYSIS DIMENSION]
[What are potential solutions or approaches?]

Step 4: [SYNTHESIS]
[What is the final recommendation or conclusion?]

Think carefully and show your reasoning at each step. Be explicit about 
assumptions and uncertainties.
```

---

### Template 23: Multi-Perspective Analysis
```
Evaluate [TOPIC/CONTENT] from multiple perspectives:

[DESCRIBE TOPIC OR PASTE CONTENT]

1. **As a [STAKEHOLDER 1]:** [What would this perspective prioritize? 
   What concerns would they have?]

2. **As a [STAKEHOLDER 2]:** [How would they view this differently?]

3. **As a [STAKEHOLDER 3]:** [What would be their main considerations?]

For each perspective:
- Key priorities
- Main concerns
- Evaluation or recommendation
- Potential conflicts with other perspectives

Then synthesize: What approach balances all perspectives?
```

---

### Template 24: Iterative Refinement
```
I need to refine this [CONTENT TYPE]:

[PASTE CONTENT]

Round 1: Evaluate the current version

Round 3: Evaluate the revision
- Did it improve?
```

## Using These Templates

### Customization Tips:

1. **Replace bracketed placeholders** with your specific information
2. **Adjust word counts** based on your needs
3. **Add or remove requirements** as appropriate
4. **Combine templates** for complex tasks
5. **Iterate and refine** based on results

### The 5 C's of Effective Prompts:

1. **Clear**: Crucial to avoid ambiguous instructions
2. **Complete**: All necessary information provided for the better results
3. **Constrained**: Specific boundaries and requirements, such as keywords, output format, certain tone or style of writing
4. **Contextualized**: Relevant background information whenever needed based on the context
5. **Constructive**: Request explanations and reasoning in your prompt skeleton

### Common Patterns to apply:

- **Role assignment**: "You are a [ROLE]..."
- **Task specification**: "Create/Write/Generate/Analyze..."
- **Context provision**: "Based on/Given/Considering..."
- **Format specification**: "Provide as a table/list/paragraph..."
- **Quality criteria**: "Ensure/Make sure/Focus on..."

## Troubleshooting on your prompts

### If outputs are too generic:
- Add more specific constraints
- Provide examples (few-shot prompting)
- Specify what to avoid
- Request unique angles or approaches

### If outputs are inaccurate:
- Request sources and citations
- Ask for step-by-step reasoning
- Verify with fact-checking prompts
- Use multiple prompts to cross-check

### If outputs don't match your voice:
- Provide examples of your preferred style
- Specify tone more precisely
- Include brand voice guidelines
- Use iterative refinement

### If outputs are too long/short:
- Specify exact word or character counts
- Request summaries or expansions
- Break into multiple prompts
- Use "expand on" or "condense" follow-ups

**Remember:** These templates are starting points. Adapt them to your specific needs, organization, and use cases. The best prompts are those refined through practice and iteration!
